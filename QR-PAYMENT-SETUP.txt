STUDY PREMIUM COURSE - SECURE UPI QR PAYMENT

1. The uploaded QR image is included at public/qr-payment.jpeg.
2. QR payments create an order with status pending_verification after the customer submits the UPI transaction/reference ID.
3. The browser can NEVER mark this order as paid.
4. Admin must open the UPI/bank app, verify the exact amount and transaction reference, then click Verify & Approve in Admin -> Orders.
5. Only a paid order can call /api/access/create and receive course access.
6. Do NOT unlock access from screenshots or UTR text alone.
7. Static UPI QR payments cannot be automatically confirmed by Cashfree. For fully automatic verification, use Cashfree's verified/dynamic payment flow.
